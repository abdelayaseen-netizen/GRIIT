# GRIIT Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

GRIIT is a challenge and habit tracking app where you commit to challenges, post proof of completion, and build streaks with others. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your controls.

## Information We Collect

- **Account information.** Your email, username, and display name, plus an optional profile photo and bio that you can add or change at any time.
- **Photos and proof content.** Photos and captions you post as proof of completing a challenge. These are captured with your permission through your camera or photo library, only when you choose to post. We never access your camera or photos in the background.
- **Location data.** Location is used only for Hard Mode tasks. When you submit proof for a Hard Mode task, we check your location once at that moment to verify the check-in. We never track your location continuously and we do not store an ongoing location history.
- **Challenge and activity data.** Your check-ins, streaks, freezes, badges, respects, comments, and follows.
- **Purchase information.** Your subscription status. Apple processes your payment and RevenueCat manages your subscription status on our behalf. We never see your payment card details.
- **Device and technical data.** Device type, operating system version, app version, your push notification token if you enable notifications, and crash reports.
- **Usage analytics.** We use PostHog to understand how the app is used so we can improve the product. We do not sell this data.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to run your challenges and streaks, display your posts to the audience you choose, verify Hard Mode check-ins, process your subscription, send notifications you have opted into, show leaderboards, fix crashes, and improve GRIIT based on how it is used.

## What Other Users Can See

Your username, display name, profile photo, bio, streaks, badges, and challenge activity are visible to other users. Proof posts follow the proof visibility setting chosen for each challenge, which can be off, optional, or public. You control what appears in each photo you post.

## Sharing With Third Parties

We share data only with the providers needed to run GRIIT:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and photo storage.
- **RevenueCat** — subscription management.
- **Apple** — payment processing.
- **PostHog** — product analytics.
- **Sentry** — crash reporting.
- **Expo** — app infrastructure and push delivery.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show third-party ads. We may disclose data if required by law.

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase cloud infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit, and access to production data is restricted.

## Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your profile, posts, photos, check-ins, and challenge history. Some data may persist briefly in backups before it is purged, and we may retain limited information where required by law. You can also request deletion by emailing us.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. We will respond to requests within 30 days. These rights may apply to you under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA where applicable.

## Children

GRIIT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, you can opt out at any time in your device settings or within the app.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date above and notify you in the app.

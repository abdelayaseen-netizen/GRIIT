128 Double-tap to respect
A double tap anywhere on a post respects it. The burst grows from the tap point: 0 to 1.2 in 180 ms on a spring (damping 12, stiffness 220), settles to 1.0 in 100 ms, holds 250 ms, then fades to 0 while shrinking to 0.9 over 200 ms, 730 ms in all, with a light haptic on the first frame. The action heart fills and pops 1 to 1.25 to 1 over 220 ms. A single tap waits 250 ms for a second tap before it opens the post. Double-tapping a post you already respect never removes the respect; only the heart icon removes it. Reduce Motion: no burst, only the fill.

76 The Secured footer offers the day, not the closing task
R3 tied the footer to the closing completion, so a day with three unshared photos that happened to end on a self-report offered nothing at all — the photos went to the record with no choice ever presented, which is the defect frames 58 and 59 exist to prevent. The footer now offers every unshared photo in the day as one set, which is also what the button already did on a multi-proof day. With nothing unshared there is nothing to offer and the footer is a single Done.

77 The last day, both outcomes
One screen, one shape, and the only thing that differs is the number and the sheet. Seventy-five squares at ten across, on the same encoding as the week strip: brand for a camera-proof day, a solid border-colour block for a self-reported one, an empty outline for a day that went unsecured. Value carries it, not opacity — surface against canvas is 1.09:1, which is no signal at all on a 33pt tile. A run with seven holes in it shows seven holes. There is no trophy, no confetti and no well done — the record is the reward, and a product that says the number is true does not decorate it.

78 Where a finished challenge lives
Two sections under the Challenges tab, Running and Finished, so a challenge that ends moves rather than disappears. Three status words and no fourth: a day count while it runs, a ratio when it ended, and "Left on day n" when the user quit. Quitting is not hidden and not punished — it is the third true thing that can happen.

How this is counted
A day is secured or it is not.
A part-done day counts for nothing.
Days
Every day you were in a challenge, from the day you joined. Today counts once it is secured. An unsecured today is never a miss.

Camera proof

Held
A freeze or a Last Stand kept the streak. A held day is not a secured day.

163 What to watch in the 5-person test
Five behaviours that tell us in 7 days whether this is working. Each is measurable from data we already log.
Day 2 return
Did all 5 open the app on day 2 before the first window closed? Who needed the push, and who came back on their own?
First share
How many people shared a proof to the feed in the first 3 days, and how long after "Keep it to the record" did they come back to share one?
Invites sent
Did anyone copy the challenge link? A test this small lives or dies on one person bringing a second.
Self-report ratio
For Photo: Optional tasks, how many are done without a photo? If it's nearly all, the photo isn't worth its friction, or the camera step is broken.
The dead feed
How many opened Home, scrolled to "You're caught up" and closed the app within 10 seconds? That is the sparse state failing.

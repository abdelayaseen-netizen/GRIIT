83 The tile: four states, three grounds, and truncation
The label sits on a scrim that fades from nothing at 42% to 78% ink at the base, so it reads white on a bright photo and on a near-black one without a box around it. The lock is a 16pt ink disc at 72%, not a bare glyph: a bare white lock disappears on a white photo and a bare dark one disappears on a dark photo, and the disc carries its own ground either way. A failed image is surface with a 1pt border and an image-off glyph — never a black square, which is indistinguishable from a photo taken in the dark. Loading is the same box filled flat with border, no label, no pulse.

114 Finish moment · no waiting wall
After "I did it" the user lands here at once. The save runs underneath, and the status line follows the server, not the tap. The share choice is on screen from the first frame, for every task type: a camera task shares its photo, and every other type shares a text card (task, challenge, Day n of N, and its gate line). A share tapped before the save lands is held and says so; if the save fails, it is dropped and the screen says nothing was shared. When the server says the day is secured, this screen hands over to the Secured moment (F), and the share block moves with it. It is never on both screens.

115 Time gate · a real picker
Times are never typed. Tapping a field opens the iOS wheel in a ds/Sheet. The field shows 12-hour time and stores HH:MM in 24-hour time. The preview row updates as the wheel moves. "Between" needs an end after the start, on the same day; an invalid window blocks Add task and says why in one line. The defaults are By 7:00 am and Between 5:00–6:30 am.

117 Profile · Proofs as a calendar
The calendar replaces the tile grid; there is no toggle. A day holds up to three photos, and the grid could only show them as loose tiles, so the calendar shows the cover photo and the v39 viewer shows the rest. Months page with the header arrows, Monday first. The line above the grid is secured over days, with no percentage. Freeze and Last Stand days are held, not secured, so they are not in the 9. Visitors see only shared photos; a day whose photos are all private shows as a plain secured cell, so the day is visible but the photo is not.

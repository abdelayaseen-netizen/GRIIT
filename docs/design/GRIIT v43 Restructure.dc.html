146 Home: slim header, Today card, the feed
Home answers two things: what is left today, and what everyone else did. The header is one line of context: the day and your name, your streak as a chip, and the bell. The Today card keeps its v42 logic (sections, gate lines, closed windows, "Done for today" per challenge) and is the only card on the screen. The feed runs underneath it. The week strip and the freeze line are gone from Home; they live on challenge detail. A new account sees Everyone, because a Friends feed for someone who follows nobody is empty. It switches to Following by default at 3 follows.

147 One post system
A proof post has the same frame whether or not there is a photo: header, the proof, the action row, then comments. A self-reported proof gets a text panel of equal visual weight instead of a photo, and no seal. Events are one compact style with an avatar every time. "Secured" is used only for days and "finished" only for challenges. Home A and B show the rhythm.

149 Discover
Search first, then the same six categories as Create. "Friends are doing" only appears when at least one friend is in a public challenge. "Popular this week" ranks challenges by joins in the last 7 days. "New from the community" lists public challenges made by users, with the creator named. Covers are generated from the category: a tint, a stripe and the category glyph, never a user's proof.

153 Add task: Photo and Limits
The five proof radios become two questions. Is a photo required, optional or not needed? Is the task limited to a time or a place? Both limits can be on. The live "On Home" row is last, so it reflects every choice above it. "Run" is out of the common chips because it is a type. The name placeholder is dimmed, starts with "e.g." and sits behind the cursor, so it reads as a hint. A Photo: Optional task opens a check-in sheet with both paths.

154 Spec sheet
Cursor builds from this and from the spec entry in cursor/02_screens.md.
Device
iPhone 16 Pro, 402 × 874. Top safe area 62. Tab bar 88 including the home indicator.
Canvas
#0F0F0F (v42 token). Surfaces #1A1917, border #2E2B27.
Type
Name 14/18 500 · body 15/20 · secondary 13/18 · caption 12/16 · label 11/14 caps 500 · heading 17/22 500 · task on a self-reported card 22/28 500. Numbers SF Pro Display Heavy 800, tabular.
Side inset
16 everywhere: cards, list rows, leaderboard, feed header. Photos are edge to edge.
Vertical rhythm
Section label 16 above, 8 below. Card padding 14. Row min-height 44; feed event 44; leaderboard 52; Discover list 64.
Radii
Cards 20 · fields, segmented, tiles in the calendar 12 / 10 · pills 999.
Avatars
80 profile · 40 notifications · 32 posts and events · 28 grouped events · 24/20 stacks. Initials fallback from v42.
Seal
28 in posts, top-right, inset 12. Camera proofs only.
Buttons
Primary 46, profile actions 36 with hitSlop to 44, inline 40.

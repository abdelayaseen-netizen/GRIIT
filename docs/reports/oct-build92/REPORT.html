Read with this in mind

  • The agents are models on a simulator, not people. Build 92's first-day group is Claude Opus and the rest Claude Sonnet; build 90 used Haiku 4.5. Part of the jump is the stronger model. The per-screen numbers (wasted taps, loops) are the fairer comparison; the Day 1 number is still the headline the build was asked to move.
  • Groups are smaller than build 90's six chunks: 12 first-day personas (build 90 had 13 fresh), 3 social groups of three, 2 history, 6 Day 2. Build 90 had no Day 2 or history sessions; it had speed, task-type, safety and words chunks that build 92 does not run.
  • Wasted taps include harness failures. Build 92's 72 wasted taps split into 64 harness (Maestro "Device became unreachable", empty or invalid model replies) and 8 app-side. Build 90's 163 split into 96 harness, 1 refused, 66 app-side (52 of those were re-taps of an already-selected radio on Start here). App-side only: 0.21 per screen visit in build 90, 0.02 in build 92.
  • Loops rose (2.20 → 3.10) because build 92 has the long social sessions: Social 5.4 per session, History 2.5, Day 2 2.3, first-day 1.8 (build 90 first-timers 2.4). The social b and c sessions hit the 60-step cap looking for a friend's post that the app does not show them (finding 3).
  • "app bug" tags in runner-part2-day2.md (d-01…d-06), on h-01 and on s-02b are harness artifacts. The day check compares the screen with the stored current_day, but the app shows the calendar day (chunk 06), and yesterday's post card says "Day 1 of 7". Every flagged screen was right.
  • The "Day 0 of 1" text from Blake's 12b dry run was the harness, not the app: its day regex read "Today" + "0 of 1 done" (Home's done count) as "Day 0 of 1". Fixed in the harness (d179cba9, with a test); the app cannot show "Day 0" (docs/reports/build92.md 13.0).
  • Words: "Rest" is not a word the app uses and the exit interview does not ask it, so it reads "not asked" in both builds.
  • The device string in result.json says "GRIIT iPhone 16"; that is a stale label in the harness. The run used the Pro Max above.

Opening a friend's running challenge from their profile always says "Challenge did not load" (s-01c steps 18–20 and 30, s-03c steps 38–45; evidence/02-visitor-challenge-did-not-load-s-01c-020.png). Steps: another person's profile → "Challenges, badges and stats" → tap a running challenge. app/profile/record.tsx line 143 pushes ROUTES.CHALLENGE_ACTIVE(row.id) for active rows, and on a visitor's record row.id is the other person's enrollment, which the viewer cannot load; Retry cannot fix it. In s-03c it was Kim's own challenge, seen through Jun's profile. Visitors should go to ROUTES.CHALLENGE_ID(row.challengeId). From chunk 07.4.
  1. An invited friend cannot see the inviter's post anywhere, which is why the 9 social sessions made 0 likes and 0 comments. The inviter's first post defaults to Friends; a Friends post shows only in the Following scope, to followers (lib/post-audience.ts audienceShowsInScope); accepting an invite does not create a follow; and the shared challenge's Recent proofs said "No shared proofs in this challenge yet" to co-members (s-01b steps 1–14, evidence/05-invitee-no-post-s-01b-005.png; same in s-02b and s-03b, which then hit the 60-step cap). Product decision: show Friends posts to co-members of the challenge, or follow on accept.
  1. Profile has no tiles for self-reported posts. h-01 (25 shared self-reported posts) sees "No shared proofs yet" (evidence/03-profile-no-self-reported-tiles-h-01-002.png). The build 92 design's ProofGrid.tsx draws a "Self-reported" tile when a post has no photo, and the app's ProfileProofs can draw one, but the server drops them: cameraProofTiles in backend/lib/proof-predicate.ts ("self-reported days emit nothing") and proofsAfterSign (a tile with no image is removed). Already noted as seen in build 91 under chunk 01; still open against 07.3.

Minor: a tap in the strip under a sheet's footer button closes the sheet and loses the draft. components/ds/Sheet.tsx gives the panel pointerEvents="box-none", so a tap on the panel outside its scroll body and buttons goes through to the full-screen Close scrim. So: harness aim, plus a small real dismiss target.

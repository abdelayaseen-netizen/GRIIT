93 B1 · one card family, five event variants
Every event is the same card: header row of avatar, name and time, then the challenge as eyebrow and the thing that happened as subject — the hierarchy the Part A day viewer already uses. The photo post is that card with an image between the two. Every card is generated from one pinned world, and a task card is addressed by challenge and task index rather than by free text: the name, the gate and the stamp all come out of that challenge's own task list, so a card cannot name a task the challenge does not have, cannot invent a gate, and cannot claim a secured day its poster did not earn.

94 B2 · comments inline, four states
Two comments at most under the engagement row, name and text on one line, so two comments cost 46pt rather than a block. Composing never happens inline: the field lives in the sheet, so the feed has one tap target for writing and one place the keyboard appears. Your own comment carries your name in brandText rather than the word "You" in a different position, so the column stays a column. The Camera and Location card is the Quick Steps run, because that is the challenge in this world whose task carries a location gate; the own-post card is yesterday's workout, the last thing this user finished on a day he still did not secure. The finish card's timestamp is derived from the challenge's end date, so the same event reads the same in every frame it appears in.

95 B3 · the per-challenge board, early in the week
Global is gone. What remains is one board per challenge, opt-in, scored on secured days since Monday with the rule written on the screen. Today is Tuesday, so exactly one day of the week has ended and the highest score anyone can hold is 1 — which is why the score carries no "of 7": a denominator of seven on a Tuesday asserts that a week nobody has lived is already assessable. The count is the number secured and the second line says how much of the week has ended. Early in the week most scores tie, so equal scores share a rank and the rule is stated rather than left to be inferred from a repeated number. The user missed Monday, so he sits at 0, which is the same Monday his own Home screen reports.

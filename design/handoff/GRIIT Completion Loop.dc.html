47 Home, a day across three challenges
One card, one section per challenge, Dividers between. Three cards would put three headers, three chips and three borders on a screen that already has a streak block above it, and law 22 is explicit that a card inside a card is out; three sibling cards is the same crowding by another route. The card is the day; the sections are the challenges. Every row leads with the same status ring, so the eye reads state down one column: brand fill and a check for done, a textSecondary ring for pending, a border ring for a closed window. No type icon, no COMPLETE chip.

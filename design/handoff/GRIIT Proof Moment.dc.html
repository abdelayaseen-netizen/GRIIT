59 Secured, a day that can hold several proofs
The hero is the streak, which is the one number that belongs to the day rather than to a challenge — so "Day 2." is gone. Day numbers are per challenge and move into the rows or the caption under the photo, where they have a name attached. Zero photos shows no image area at all and lists the challenges instead; one shows it full width; three or more show a row of three with a +n, because a contact sheet of six thumbnails at this size is a texture, not a record. When the task that closed the day carried an unshared photo, the share choice lands here rather than on a frame 58 the user never sees — the two screens are never shown back to back, so the choice moves onto whichever one is reached.

60 Profile, Proofs, and the full view
A 3-up grid of square tiles under a date header, newest first, the challenge name burned into the tile corner. Not "Day 13": that is the ambiguity frame 59 removed, and a day can hold a Day 13 and a Day 2 at once. The date is stated once per section and the challenge once per tile, so every tile is answerable on its own. Self-reported days do not appear, because there is no photo to show and a placeholder tile would be a picture of a proof that does not exist — but the count under the grid names them, so the grid is never mistaken for the whole record. The full view is headed by the date for the same reason and carries the challenge and its day number in the body, where there is room to say both.

Onboarding rules
Length
6 screens before you’re in a challenge: Welcome, Goals, How it works, Start here, Reminder, Account. Then Home.
Removed
“Set your line” is gone. The length always comes from the challenge.
One rule
Every example obeys “every task done = day secured”. No “2 of 3” anywhere.
Start here
Only public, joinable challenges that match the goals. Invite-only never shows. Each card says what you do every day.
Joining
Join starts Day 1 today, as a guest. The account step keeps it; sign-up merges the guest enrollment and proofs.
Skip
Skip on goals or how it works jumps to Start here; on Start here it lands on Browse all. Reminder and Account can’t be skipped past without a choice.
Browse all
Same flat rows and Join buttons as Start here. No cover grid in onboarding.
Orange
The primary button and the flame only.

The Done button, every task type
One word
Every task finishes with a button that starts “Done”. What follows says what it does.
Self-reported
No button: one tap on the circle in Today. Undo toast for 5 s.
Timer
“Start timer” primary; “Done, already did it” as a text button. Logged as self-reported.
Counter
“Done · 30 pages” sets the count to the target and saves. “Log n of N” for partial.
Photo
“Done · take photo” opens the camera; saving the photo is the completion.
Then
routeAfterSave: toast, Secured or FinishMoment (v48.1 rule).

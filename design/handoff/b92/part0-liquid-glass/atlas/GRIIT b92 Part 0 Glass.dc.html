Create
Behaviour
Scrolling down shrinks the bar to the selected tab. Scrolling up, or a tap on it, brings the full bar back.
+ reachable
While shrunk, “+ Create” stays on screen as the bar accessory (inline) and opens the same chooser (P0-14). Hidden when the full bar is showing, because Create is the middle tab.
Reduce Motion
No shrink. The bar stays at full size, so Create is always the middle tab.
Engineering
Confirm the accessory can hide in the expanded placement. If not, keep it showing in both and remove Create from the tabs.

2.1 Profile header · own and visitor
Option A recommended.
Where Challenges, Badges and Stats go · 2 options
A · Record row
One row under the buttons: “Challenges, badges and stats” + a summary line. Visible, labelled, one tap. Costs 64 pt above the grid. Recommended.
B · Top-bar icon
A chart icon next to Settings. More of the grid shows, but an unlabelled icon is easier to miss.
Both open
“Your record”: Challenges · Badges · Stats. Streak history, per-challenge progress and badges are free; only deeper stats are Pro.
The 3 profile numbers · one source each
Streak
Days in a row secured, up to today. Today counts once secured; yesterday unsecured and not held → 0. user_stats.current_streak
Best
The longest streak ever, the same run as the “Best 18 · Sep 20 – Oct 7” record. user_stats.best_streak
Days secured
Total secured days, all time. user_stats.secured_days
Verified
Under Days secured: secured days where every task had camera or Strava proof. user_stats.verified_days
Proofs
Not on the profile header. Shown once, as the count on the Proofs heading in Your record. user_stats.proof_count
Followers · following
One quiet line. follows table counts. No Friends number.
Rule
Home, Your record, challenge pages and share cards read these same fields. Never computed on the client.

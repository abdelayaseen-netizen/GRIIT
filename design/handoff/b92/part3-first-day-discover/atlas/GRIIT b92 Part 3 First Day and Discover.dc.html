3.5 Words
Words · one definition each, shown once at the moment, then an “i”
Secured
Every task today is done, so the day counts toward your streak. First shown: the first Day secured (Part 1).
Freeze
Covers one missed day so your streak doesn’t break. You earn one every 7 days in a row. First shown: the first freeze earned (P3-22).
Self-reported
You marked it done yourself, with no photo or app proof. First shown: the first self-reported task (P3-25).
Verified
Proven by the camera or Strava. First shown: the first verified proof (badge “i”).

GRIIT build 92 · Part 1
The finish loop
From Done on any task to back on Home: one post sheet, then Day secured, a toast, or Challenge complete. Stickers, what posts show, likes, proof detail and the input rules. Glass chrome from Part 0. iPhone SE and iPhone 16 Pro Max.

1.1 One finish flow
One finish flow · the rule
1 · Done
On any task. Photo tasks open the camera first; the post sheet opens after the photo.
2 · Post sheet
Who sees this (Friends default · Everyone · Only me), photo/result + optional caption, primary names the choice. The only place the audience is asked.
3 · Then, one of
Finished the whole challenge → Challenge complete (P1-10/11). Else last task today → Day secured (P1-06–09). Else → Home with a toast and the next task highlighted (P1-05).
Never
The Proof screen in this flow. Proof opens only from the feed or the profile.
Short of goal
Saved and postable, labelled “n of N · x short”. The task stays open, today isn’t secured, no Day secured.

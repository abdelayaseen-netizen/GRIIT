What changed and why
Profile like Instagram
Grid of 4:5 tiles (Today first, self-reported tiles honest) → a vertical feed of days, newest first. A two-proof day swipes with “1 of 2”. Every proof names challenge, task, gate and Shared or Private; private ones offer “Share this proof”.
Feed and posts
Full-bleed cards, double-tap respect, counts hidden at 0, the camera seal explained in a sheet, comments in a sheet with posting, failed and delete states. Report (not built) is a reason picker and a confirmation.
Discover
Covers only from the generator; six categories now separate by colour alone (tints raised in chroma). Search covers challenges and people. Sparse, loading, error and no-results states.
Challenge you’re in
Day hero, “days done · secured”, the same freeze row as Home, This week board (only in groups), recent proofs, record, invite. Leave takes effect at midnight.
Groups
Roster with today’s state per person and the group streak defined. Invite picker with a link. Invite accept, expired, full. Nudge from three fixed lines, and what the recipient sees.
Decisions applied
Strictness is “Strict”. Leaving takes effect tomorrow. Photos are varied real-looking proofs.

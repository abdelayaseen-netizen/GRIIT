98 Story placement · 1080 × 1920 at 30%
The story is 1080 × 1920. Stickers are placed inside the band between 250 px from the top and 340 px from the bottom; Meta publishes no exact figure, so these are the working margins for the progress bar and profile above and the reply bar below. When GRIIT sets the background itself (card or photo), the sticker sits at the bottom of the safe band, horizontally centred, 810 px wide at 1080 — 75% of the width, never full-bleed. When the user places a transparent sticker on their own photo, Instagram lets them move it; GRIIT only sets the default.

99 Share sheet · preview, background, four actions
One ds/Sheet for all three variants. The preview is the exact PNG that will be exported, shown on a checkerboard when transparent. Background is a three-way choice; Photo offers only photos answered Share — a Keep photo is never offered or named as an option to make public (R3), so for a day whose proof is private the segment is disabled with one line saying why. Instagram Story hands the sticker and background to Instagram; Copy puts the PNG on the clipboard; Save writes it to Photos; More opens the iOS share sheet. With nothing secured there is nothing to render, and every action is inert.

100 Verified mark · a check and the proof type
The VERIFIED pill becomes a 14pt brand disc with an ink check, followed by the proof type in brandText: Camera, or Camera · Place once location verification ships (until then Place never appears). The word "Verified" is gone from the product. Self-reported gets no disc and no colour — the words alone, in textSecondary. The mark sits in the card's meta line beneath the photo, never on it, so it needs no scrim in the app; on stickers it takes the same stroke and shadow as the rest of the sticker.

Apple Health rules
Label
“From Apple Health”, plus the source app when known (“From Apple Health · Strava”). Never “Verified”, never the camera seal.
Sources
No direct Strava, Garmin or Nike connection. They write into Apple Health; GRIIT reads Apple Health only.
Metrics
Steps (daily total) · Workout minutes (any type, summed for the day) · Distance (one run or walk). Chosen in Create.
Matching
First qualifying workout inside the task window completes the task once. Steps complete when the day’s total passes the target, checked on app open and by background delivery.
Permission
Pre-prompt, then iOS. iOS never tells apps that read access was denied, so “Off” means “asked, but nothing readable”.
Fallback
While it’s off or nothing is readable, the task asks for a photo instead. The day stays securable.
Feed
A recorded run shares as a card with distance, time, pace and the label. Photo optional.
Create: a task proved from Apple Health
Pick the metric, see the permission sheet once, and what happens when Health isn’t sharing or has nothing for today.

Create rules for Apple Health tasks
Metric
Steps (daily total) · Workout minutes (any type, added up) · Distance (one run or walk, km or mi from Region).
Target
One number. Steps 1,000–50,000 · minutes 5–300 · distance 0.5–100.
Permission
Choosing From Apple Health the first time opens the sheet; Continue → iOS. Later choices skip it.
Not sharing
Continue stays off; offer Open Health or Use a photo instead.
No data today
The task stays open with “No data yet”; never a miss before 11:59 pm.
Copy
“From Apple Health” everywhere. Never “Verified”.

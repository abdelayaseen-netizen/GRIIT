179 Group space inside challenge detail
A group's detail page is its room. The "Today" roster lists every member with where they are today (secured, not yet with a count, window closed) and their own streak. It is ordered you first, then by name, never by score, so it is a roll call and not a ranking. The board under it stays top 3 plus you. Posts are only proofs members chose to share, from this challenge. The invite row sits at the top with the head count, because a group that isn't full is still recruiting.

180 Nudge
Nudge appears only on a member who has not secured today and can still secure it, and only to someone in the same challenge. Each person can be nudged once per day per sender; after that the button reads "Nudged". A line is optional and comes from three, with no free text, so there is nothing to moderate. "2 hours left." is offered only when it is true for the recipient. The recipient gets a push and a line on top of their Today card that leads straight to the task.

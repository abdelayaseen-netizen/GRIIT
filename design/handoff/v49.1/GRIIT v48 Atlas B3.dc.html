What changed and why
Someone else’s profile
Same header and grid as your own. Visitors see shared proofs only, in the same day feed. Eight relationship states, each with one button.
Activity
Every notification type with its copy and destination. Group invites accept inline. Boards: challenge boards only, ranked, unranked with neighbours, ties, solo, and a chip row that scrolls instead of clipping.
Create
StepBasics with a live name check and the generated cover. Packs collapse and expand. The add-task sheet keeps its last field clear of the sticky button. Same-day wheel picker.
Settings
Account with the full delete flow and the Apple subscription warning. Privacy as one switch with a who-sees-what table and a stranger preview. Blocked users, permissions and quiet hours. “Circle activity” is now “Group activity”.
Paywall
USD examples, every string from RevenueCat. The billed amount is the biggest price; the monthly equivalent is secondary. Every purchase state.
Batch 2 decisions
Tints in tokens; no map; a freeze holds the group streak; creator ownership passes to the longest-standing member; Delete removes from the feed only. The phone owner is Yaseen Abdelaziz (@yaseen) everywhere.

Out before six. The street was empty except for the man who sweeps the lot behind the bakery, and he nodded like he had been expecting me. Twelve days in and the part that changed is not the legs, it is that I stopped negotiating at four in the morning. The alarm goes and the argument does not start.
Yesterday I wanted to skip the second session, so I wrote down why instead of deciding. Reading it back it was three excuses about the weather wearing one coat. I went anyway. Ninety minutes, nothing heroic, and the rest of the day came easier because the hard part was already behind me and nobody had to talk me into it.
The reading is what I still fight. Ten pages is nothing until the phone is in reach, and then it is a wall. Leaving it on the counter overnight has worked four days out of five.

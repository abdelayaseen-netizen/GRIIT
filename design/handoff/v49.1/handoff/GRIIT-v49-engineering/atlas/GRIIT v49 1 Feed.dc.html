Feed post rules
Header
Avatar 40 · name (Headline) · @handle · time. The handle truncates before the name. Tap the time for the full date; the thread always shows it.
Context
One line: challenge (Text 1) · Day n of N · task. Tap opens the challenge.
Body
Caption, then a 4:5 photo with the camera seal, a self-reported card, or an Apple Health card. Never a bare text post.
Actions
Respect · Comment · Share with counts. Zero shows no number. Respected = filled icon in Text 1, never orange.
Open
Tapping anywhere except an action opens the thread.
Activity
“Started X”, “joined X” lines are grouped per challenge per hour, 13pt Text 2 with 18pt faces. Never a card, never above a proof.
Share count
Counts card shares and copied links. Decision 218.

Photo zoom rules
Open
Tap the photo in the feed or thread. The photo grows from its spot in the feed.
Close
Swipe down past 120pt or flick; X top left. Returns to the same scroll spot.
Several
Swipe sideways. “1 of 2” top centre plus dots; the feed post opens on the photo you tapped.
Chrome
Name, context line and actions sit on a bottom fade. Tap the photo to hide them.
Zoom
Pinch up to 3×, double-tap 2×. Sideways swipe is off while zoomed in.

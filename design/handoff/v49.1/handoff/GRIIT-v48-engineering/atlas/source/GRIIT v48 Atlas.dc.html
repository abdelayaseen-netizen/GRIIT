What changed and why
Streak first
Home opens on the flame, the number and the week strip. The date, the name and the “Today” title are gone; the strip is one button to StreakSheet.
Primary on top
The one orange button sits under the status line, so it can never hide behind the tab bar. Today follows, then the feed.
Safe areas
Every frame draws the island, the status bar and the home indicator. Five frames carry a measured overlay: tab, pushed, step, sheet, camera.
Feed like Instagram
Full-width 4:5 photos, edge to edge. Multi-photo days swipe with a “1 of 2” pill. Self-reported posts are compact rows. “Started” lines group, at most one group per four posts.
One sentence each
Every Home state says what happened and what’s next in one line. The freeze message is one string. “Today can’t be secured” appears once.
Status bar = data
Times follow the Up by 5 window: before 5:30 while it’s open, later once it’s closed.
Strips
Area 3 is drawn as strips from the tap to the landing screen, with every arrow naming the tap.
Less
Removed from Home: date, name, “Days done” count, week count, first-day takeover. Removed everywhere: orange text, the unread orange dot.

192 · Week strip · unchanged, approved in batch 1
The week strip: eight states, readable without colour
Circles are 30pt with 13pt glyphs, and day letters sit above them. Every state differs by fill, outline or glyph, so it reads in greyscale. Secured is white, not orange: a week of secured days keeps the squint test. A held day is raised and carries its glyph, so it reads as protected rather than missed. A missed day is an outline and a dash, never red and never ×. The whole strip is one button that opens StreakSheet.

What changed and why
Delayed sign-up
Welcome → WhyProof → WhyCircle → Goals → DayTarget → FirstChallenge → Reminders → Account → Profile. Account comes after the first challenge so day one isn’t gated.
Real UI as illustration
WhyProof and WhyCircle use the actual task rows, seal and roster, not paragraphs.
Honest permission prompts
One “Continue” before the iOS alert, a designed denied state, and a skip that works.
Auth
Apple first, then Google and email. Inline field errors, in-button loading, the 60 s resend lock, an expired-link state.
System
Offline, error, not found, force update, confirm, toasts, pull to refresh, loading rules, every push on the lock screen, splash and icon.
Index
Every frame across the four batches, linked, with source and Built in 75. A retire list closes out legacy routes.

The streak and the consistency ratio merge into one row and both point at Consistency. The alternative keeps them as two cards. The cost is 76pt, and on a 852pt screen that is the difference between the first row of proof days landing above the fold and below it — which is the whole point of making Proofs primary.

88 A1 and A2 · the profile, owner and visitor
Proofs is the default tab and the unit is a day, not a photo: date, photo count, and the day's first photo as cover. A day carries the lock when any photo in it is private, so the owner can see at a glance which days hold something nobody else has seen. The visitor profile contains shared photos only and days with none do not appear at all — so the grid never hints at a private photo by leaving a gap where one would be.

92 A4 · badges as rows
A4 asked for a treatment change, so the five marks, their requirements, their earned dates and the footnote are exactly the ones already shipping in frame 21. Rows, not a two-column tile grid: the requirement line is the part that says what the mark costs, and in a tile it had nowhere to go. The stamp language holds — no icons, no circles, no cards — so earned and unearned differ on three channels, not opacity: the stamp border and its letters are brandText against border and textSecondary, and the trailing word says which.

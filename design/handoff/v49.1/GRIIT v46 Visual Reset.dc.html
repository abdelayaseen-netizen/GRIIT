187 Discover: typographic covers
Every challenge has a cover built from its category tint, its duration and its category icon, so no image slot is ever empty. Featured is a row of 160 × 200 cards; a category is a two-column 4:5 grid. The meta line is always "{duration} · {gate}". "Hard" is an outline chip, and "in it" appears only when someone is. The People row leaves you out and shows each person's streak or day.

Greeting rules
Where
Above the streak block. The streak stays the first number on screen.
Copy
“Good morning / afternoon / evening, Yaseen”. 22:00–3:59: “Still up, Yaseen”. First name only; falls back to @handle. No emoji, no exclamation marks.
Layout
20pt title + one line that says today’s move; it replaces the “1 of 2 left today” line, so Home gains only 24pt.
First week
Days before you joined never show as misses. The strip counts days 1–7 until day 8.

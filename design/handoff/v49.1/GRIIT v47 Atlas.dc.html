192 · Week strip
The week strip: eight states, readable without colour
Circles are 30pt with 13pt glyphs, and day letters sit above them. Every state differs by fill, outline or glyph, so it reads in greyscale. Secured is white, not orange: a week of secured days keeps the squint test. A held day is raised and carries its glyph, so it reads as protected rather than missed. A missed day is an outline and a dash, never red and never ×. The whole strip is one button that opens StreakSheet.

82 Profile, the four statuses
One word per status the table already has: active is a day position, completed is a ratio, abandoned is "Left on day n", failed is "Failed on day n". Failed is the blunt word on purpose — hard mode's entire contract is that one unsecured day ends the run, and softening the word after the fact would be the app apologising for a rule the user chose. Nothing is coloured by outcome: a 75 of 75 and a Failed row are the same textSecondary.

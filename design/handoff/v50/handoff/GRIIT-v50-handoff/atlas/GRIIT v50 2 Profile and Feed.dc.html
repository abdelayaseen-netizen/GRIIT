Proof scroll rules
Order
Newest to oldest, one entry per proof. Two tasks on one day are two entries with the same date.
Open
Starts at the tapped tile; scroll up for newer, down for older.
Pager
Several photos swipe sideways, “1 of 2” top right + dots. Vertical scroll never pages.
Private
Lock tag, “Only you can see this.”, no counts.
Shared
Tag + respect and comment counts; tap counts to open the thread.
Back
Back or swipe right returns to the grid at the last viewed tile.

Home top rules (all options)
One hero
The card: streak, week and today’s move in one surface. Nothing outside it is bigger than Text 2 15pt.
One action
One orange primary: the next open task. It becomes “Use a freeze” when yesterday is unsecured, and a quiet “Share today” once the day is secured.
Numeral
SF Pro Display 700, tabular, −0.025em, 32pt (30pt at 1,000+). Thousands get a comma.
Best
“Best {n}” when the current streak is below the best; “Your best” when it equals or beats it. Never “Best” alone.
Plurals
“1 day in a row”, “14 days in a row” via count().
First week
Days 1–7 label the strip until day 8 (v49 rule).
Orange
Flame and the primary only. The strip stays Text 1 / greys.

The density pass
Frames 67 to 74. Every pair below is the same markup rendered through two token sets, so the only variable is the scale, and every number in the notes is measured off these frames rather than estimated. Home, Feed and Profile are clipped at the tab bar, which is what the phone does: every clip derives its height from the bar above it, so nothing in these frames sits under the bar and law 26 holds in all six. The Barlow Condensed numerals do not move: they are the one thing in this app that should be larger than the competition, and shrinking them would spend the identity to save forty points.
